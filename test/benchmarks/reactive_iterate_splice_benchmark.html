## Reactive: Iteration Splice [reactive_iterate_splice_benchmark.html](reactive_iterate_splice_benchmark.html) replaces the middle element of an existing list with `splice(index, 1, item)`. It measures lists of 100, 1,000 and 10,000 items without changing their length. Unlike Granular Update, the item itself is replaced, not one of its properties. The benchmark measures the current rendering strategy; constant-time updates and preservation of DOM node identity are not assumed. Info: Output is hidden. Replace one list element without changing list length.
