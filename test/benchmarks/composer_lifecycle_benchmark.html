## DOM Lifecycle [composer_lifecycle_benchmark.html](composer_lifecycle_benchmark.html) runs five batches of 20 create/update/remove cycles after one warmup cycle. Each cycle creates 100 text bindings against the same long-lived reactive model. It reports separate accumulated times per batch for mounting/rendering, updating all bindings and removing the view including cleanup. This exercises Composer's DOM observation, rendering and cleanup together with reactive subscription registration/release. It does not measure application module `dock`/`undock` hooks or condition toggling. Template construction and pauses between operations are excluded. Info: Output is hidden. Repeated DOM creation, reactive update and removal.
